Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

The short version

  • NoteTube runs entirely in your browser. There is no NoteTube server, database, or account system.
  • Your notes stay on your device. They are saved in your browser’s local storage, and NoteTube never receives them.
  • No analytics, ads, or tracking. NoteTube doesn’t collect, sell, or share personal information.
  • Google is involved in two places: the embedded YouTube player, and Google Docs export (only if you use it). Google’s own privacy policy applies to those.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the NoteTube web app (the “app”), a tool for watching YouTube videos and taking notes alongside them. The words “we” and “us” refer to the people who operate the app.

What stays on your device

NoteTube saves the following in your browser’s localStorage and sessionStorage so the app works the way you left it. This data never leaves your device unless you export it yourself.

Data stored in your browser
Data Why
Your notes, one set per video Autosave, so your notes are there when you come back
Recently opened video IDs and titles The “Recent” menu
Last playback position for each video Resuming where you left off
Preferences: theme, panel sizes, “pause while typing” Remembering your settings
A Google access token (session storage, only if you export to Google Docs) Avoiding a new sign-in on every export. It expires after about an hour and is cleared when you close the tab.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. That also deletes your notes permanently, so export anything you want to keep first.

NoteTube does not set cookies.

Third-party services

YouTube (Google)

Videos play in YouTube’s embedded player, which loads directly from YouTube. When you load a video, YouTube receives information such as your IP address, the video you’re watching, and your interactions with the player. YouTube may also set its own cookies. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. By using the player you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Google sign-in and Google Drive (optional)

If you choose Export → Google Docs, you sign in with Google in a popup and grant NoteTube permission to create files in your Google Drive. NoteTube requests only the drive.file permission. That lets it create new files and access only the files it creates, never the rest of your Drive.

Your notes then go straight from your browser to Google Drive. They never pass through a NoteTube server. NoteTube doesn’t request or receive your name, email address, or profile.

NoteTube’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The access token is used only to create the document you asked for. It is never stored outside your browser, shared, sold, or used for advertising.

You can sign out from the Export menu. You can also remove NoteTube’s access entirely at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Hosting

The app’s files are served by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel may process standard request information (such as IP address, browser type, and the time of the request) to deliver the site and keep it secure. NoteTube does not use this information for analytics.

Exports

Text, Markdown, Word, and PDF exports are created in your browser and downloaded directly to your device. “Copy link” writes a YouTube link to your clipboard only when you click it.

Children

NoteTube is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the way NoteTube handles data changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date. For example, if a server or sync feature were ever added, it would be described here before launch.
